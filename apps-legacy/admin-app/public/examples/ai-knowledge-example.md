# Hours and payment

We are open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
Closed on public holidays — check our Instagram for special hours.

We accept cash, debit/credit cards, and QRIS.
Minimum card payment is Rp 25.000.
Split bills are available on request.

Oat milk and almond milk are available for any drink.
Our signature item is the house latte with local beans.
